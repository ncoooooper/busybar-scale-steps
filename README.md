# Scale Steps

Practise scale degrees in seconds, thirds or fourths and transpose the same exercise into another key.

Keep a single note and scale-degree marker in view while you play. Transposing the exercise keeps the interval pattern intact, so you can repeat it in a second key without writing out a new note list.

## Practise a scale in thirds

1. Hold the wheel to choose major or natural minor as needed. Press **Start** to cycle seconds, thirds and fourths. A change of exercise returns to its first position.
2. Turn one step at a time and play the displayed note on your instrument. The rear gives the key, exercise and degree.
3. In the recording the exercise begins on C4 and moves to E4. The highlighted marker follows the scale degree.

![The note changes from C4 to E4 and the active scale-degree marker moves.](media/guide/practice-in-thirds.gif)

## Transpose the same pattern

1. Click once to put the wheel in transposition mode. Turn to shift the tonic a semitone per step.
2. Check the new key on the rear. Click again to make the wheel advance through the exercise rather than changing key.
3. Play the next shown note. The clip passes through F4 and F♯4, then advances to A4 in the transposed pattern.

![The reference pitch changes through F4 and F#4 before the next step displays A4.](media/guide/transpose-the-exercise.gif)

## Example from the emulator

In C major the opening thirds go C4, E4, G4. Transpose that same exercise up two semitones for D4, F♯4, A4. The front is a visual pitch prompt; the sound comes from the instrument you are practising.

## Run in the emulator

Open BUSY Bar Emulator. From this repository, run:

```sh
~/.busy-emulator/bin/busy-emulator dev --once
```

Press **Back** to exit.
